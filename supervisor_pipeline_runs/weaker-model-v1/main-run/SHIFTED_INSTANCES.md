# Shifted instances (at least one gpt-5.5 run ranks the gold below first)

Generated by `scripts/mr_report.py` from the frozen per-run measures (`analysis/per_run.json`). KPI rule: a run is a SHIFT when it submitted a ranking and the gold is not ranked first; an instance succeeds when at least one of its first 5 valid runs shifts. Failure modes are mechanical labels from frozen fields (rules in the script docstring), not blind-coded.

**50 of 67** validated instances tested have at least one shifted run (194/335 answered runs shifted).

**Pattern across batch1 (coordinator, hypothesis).** All 21 shifted runs have the same form. The agent displays the gold's fault line, reads the gold in full and runs a test, then ranks the facade (the entry point the trigger test calls) first and the gold 2nd (17 runs), 3rd (3 runs) or 4th (1 run). No run ranks an intermediate hop first, no run fails to reach the gold, and no run times out. The shift rate follows the type of omission. It is high when the gold, without the omitted step, still reads as a complete routine for ordinary input: a plain sum with no NaN skip (Math-3 5/5, Chart-2 3/5), a high-surrogate match with no low-surrogate check (Lang-14 5/5), or a whitespace skip with no word-state reset (Lang-45 5/5). It is low when the omission leaves a visibly broken routine: a copy loop whose index never advances (Lang-39 0/5), an argmax that never records its index (Lang-16 1/5), or an accumulator returned without its fallback, so the result is null for every ordinary value (Lang-1 1/5). Lang-22 (1/5) falls between the two: the dropped loop bound is a cap that the gold's own javadoc states. Whether the gold's javadoc states the convention does not by itself separate the two groups, because Math-3's gold javadoc says 'sum of the finite entries' and it still shifted 5/5.

**Pattern across batch2 (coordinator, hypothesis).** Batch 2 used the W26 preference for special-case omissions, yet only 5 of 10 instances shifted (17/50 runs), against 7 of 8 in batch 1. The 16 entry-point runs again ranked the facade first with the gold displayed and read in full. In three of them (Lang-7 runs 3 and 5, Closure-10 run 4) the ranking simply follows the call chain from the top (facade, hop 1 to hop 4, gold 6th). One Closure-10 run (run-003) never displayed the gold's fault line. Refined hypothesis across both batches: an instance shifts when the omission leaves NO LOCAL TRACE in the gold, and rarely shifts when it leaves one. A local trace is any of: (1) a parameter or local variable that is never read, or is written but never updated (Lang-32 `useTransients` never read; Lang-26 `locale` passed in but missing from the cache key; Lang-1 the computed `head` unused; Lang-16 `index` never updated; Lang-39 `index` never advanced); (2) a missing sibling case in an enumeration the gold visibly handles (Chart-11 SEG_QUADTO missing beside MOVETO/LINETO/CUBICTO; JacksonCore-5 `~1` missing beside `~0`; Lang-11 a `numbers`-only branch missing beside the `letters`-only branch); (3) a rule that the gold's own javadoc states (Lang-22's 10,000 cap). With this reading, all 9 no-trace instances shifted at least once (Lang-14, Lang-45, Math-3, Lang-7, Closure-10 5/5; Chart-2, Lang-30, Lang-63 3/5; Lang-15 1/5), and only 3 of the 9 traced instances did, each at 1/5 (Lang-1, Lang-16, Lang-22). This classification was made after seeing the results and must be tested prospectively.

**Pattern across batch3 (coordinator, hypothesis).** Batch 3 was built under the frozen no-local-trace rule (W29) and run in seven cost-guarded blocks. The pre-registered prediction reads SUPPORTED: 15 of 17 instances shifted at least once (s = 0.88, threshold 0.80), 61/85 runs (72%), against 12/18 instances and 38/90 runs (42%) in batches 1 and 2. Both W31 secondary subsets also read SUPPORTED: 14/15 when Lang-46 and Lang-65 are dropped (majority of 3 raters NO_TRACE), and 12/13 on the unanimous subset. Every shifted run ranked the facade first (61/61): the gold came 2nd in 54, 3rd in 1, 4th in 2 and 6th in 4, and the gold was read in full in 57. Caveats. Batch 3 differs from batches 1-2 in more than the rule: other bugs, older Lang versions, and a construction pipeline with a blind checker and one redesign. There is no control, so this is a prospective consistency check of the hypothesis, not a causal test. Five instances had a single shift in batches 1-2; in batch 3, Lang-57 had one and Lang-35 and Lang-51 two each.

**Pattern across batch4 (coordinator, hypothesis).** Batch 4 replicated batch 3 under the unchanged no-local-trace rule: 11 of 12 instances shifted at least once (s = 0.92, pre-registered reading REPLICATES), 48/60 runs. Both robustness subsets also replicate (11/11 with the 3-rater majority NO_TRACE, 9/9 unanimous). All 48 shifted runs ranked the facade first, with the gold displayed in all 48 and read in full in 47; the gold came 2nd in 40 runs and 3rd to 7th in 8 (Lang-21 and Lang-52 rank the chain from the facade down). The only instance without a shift, Math-85, is the one gold whose two extra raters both found a trace (T4). Across batches 3 and 4, the two never-shifting golds (Lang-65, Math-85) are exactly the two whose 3-rater majority was TRACE. This is post-hoc and descriptive. Caveats as before: different bugs and pipeline from batches 1-2, no control arm.

**Pattern across batch5 (coordinator, hypothesis).** The two new projects diverge: JacksonDatabind shifted on 1 of 6 instances (5 of 30 runs) and Time on 5 of 8; Lang 3 of 3 and the two Closure reuses 2 of 2 shifted as in batches 1-4. Every shifted run but one ranked the facade first (the entry point); Time-9 run-002 ranked an upstream hop first. The record does not attribute the per-project difference: project, facade kind, host sizes and the facade reuse status are confounded (W47 addendum).
